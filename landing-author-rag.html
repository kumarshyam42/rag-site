FAQ

Common questions

What if the AI makes up answers that aren't in my content?
It won't. AuthorRAG uses strict retrieval-augmented generation — every answer is grounded in your actual content with source citations. If your content doesn't cover a topic, the system says so rather than guessing.
Do I retain ownership of my content?
Absolutely. You own all your intellectual property. We index it for search purposes only. Your content is never shared with other authors, used for AI training, or made public. Delete your account and everything is removed.
How do my clients access the platform?
You share a link to your branded knowledge library (e.g., library.yourname.com). You control access — invite by email, require a subscription, or make it open. It works on any device, no app download needed.
How long does setup take?
Most authors are live within 5-7 business days. You send us your content, we handle the technical setup — parsing, indexing, branding, and deployment. You review and approve before launch.
Can I see what clients are searching for?
Yes. Your analytics dashboard shows popular queries, trending topics, content gaps (questions your content doesn't cover well), and usage patterns. Many authors use this data to plan their next book or course.
